# Contributing and collaboration

Thank you for your interest in LX Obsidian Labs.

This repository is the **engineering profile and evidence index**, not a shared codebase for all projects. To propose a code change, open an issue or pull request in the relevant project's repository and follow that project's own instructions and license.

## Documentation corrections

For a broken link, inaccurate project status, unclear attribution or documentation defect:

1. Identify the affected page or repository and include a direct link.
2. Explain what is inaccurate and provide reproducible evidence where possible.
3. Propose a concise correction that separates verified facts from assumptions.
4. Do not publish private customer information, credentials or proprietary materials.

## Engineering contributions

- Open a focused issue before proposing large changes or new features.
- Keep pull requests small and explain the problem, solution, risks and testing performed.
- Record exact test commands and outcomes. Do not claim tests passed unless executed.
- Preserve upstream copyright notices, licenses and original author attribution.
- Avoid committing generated artifacts, build outputs or secrets unless explicitly required and approved.
- Do not modify client-owned content or branding without the relevant approval.

## Security reports

Do not disclose vulnerabilities in public issues. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting.

## Commercial and client work

A public repository does not automatically grant permission to reuse client trademarks, media, business data or other assets. Verify licensing and ownership before reuse. For commercial enquiries, contact [vilanenathan@gmail.com](mailto:vilanenathan@gmail.com).

## Evidence standard

Portfolio claims follow the [evaluation rubric](PORTFOLIO_EVALUATION.md) and [engineering evidence register](ENGINEERING_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md). Development stage, test verification and engineering quality are distinct signals.
